LIFT LOG 1.8 - WEAR OS COMPANION

What you get
- Your previously logged exercises first, most recently logged first.
- Last weight and date, including histories for exercises hidden on the phone.
- Large minus/plus controls, exact entry by tapping the weight, adjustable step.
- New entries saved locally on the watch, then added to the phone's full history.
- All exercises by muscle group as a secondary option; hidden exercises stay hidden there.
- Bezel/crown scrolling when the watch supplies rotary input events.

Requires a Wear OS 3 or newer watch paired to your Android phone. Designed for
Galaxy Watch4 Classic / Watch6 Classic; the user's exact model is not confirmed.
Not for old Tizen Galaxy watches or Apple Watch. Keep Google Play services updated.

FIRST: UPDATE YOUR PHONE
1. Download LiftLog.apk 1.8 on your phone.
2. Install it OVER the existing Lift Log. Do not uninstall or clear storage.
3. Open Lift Log. Check your saved weights are present.

SECOND: INSTALL ON THE WATCH (WINDOWS)
Opening LiftLog-Watch.apk on the phone does NOT install it on the watch.
Use the included Windows installer, or use ADB manually as described below.

1. Extract ALL of LiftLog-Watch-Setup.zip into a folder on your Windows PC.
2. Connect the PC and watch to the same Wi-Fi network.
3. On the watch: Settings > About watch > Software information.
   Tap Software version repeatedly (usually five times) until Developer mode
   is enabled. Menu names can vary by watch software version.
4. Return to Settings > Developer options. Enable ADB debugging and Wireless
   debugging (older watch software calls it Debug over Wi-Fi).
5. Double-click Install-Watch.cmd in the extracted folder.
6. Follow its prompts. If your watch has Pair new device:
   - Enter the pairing IP:port from Pair new device and its pairing code.
   - Go BACK to the Wireless debugging screen for the CONNECTION IP:port.
   - The connection port usually differs from the pairing port.
7. Allow debugging on the watch if prompted. The installer checks that the
   selected device is a watch before installing. It does not uninstall anything.
8. Open Lift Log on both devices. On the watch, scroll down and tap Sync with
   phone. On the phone, Watch companion > Sync with watch is also available.
9. Once your logged exercises appear, disable ADB and Wireless debugging.
   Normal Lift Log sync does not require debugging to remain enabled.

MANUAL ADB (WINDOWS / MAC / LINUX)
Use Google's Android SDK Platform-Tools. On Windows, the included platform-tools
folder contains the official Windows tools and their notices.
Replace the IP addresses and ports below with the ones displayed by your watch.

adb pair WATCH_IP:PAIRING_PORT
adb connect WATCH_IP:CONNECTION_PORT
adb -s WATCH_IP:CONNECTION_PORT shell pm list features
  Verify output includes feature:android.hardware.type.watch before continuing.
adb -s WATCH_IP:CONNECTION_PORT install -r LiftLog-Watch.apk
adb -s WATCH_IP:CONNECTION_PORT shell am start -n com.axs.gymtrack/.WatchActivity

Older watch software with only Debug over Wi-Fi: skip pair and use the address
shown on the watch (commonly port 5555); approve the prompt on the watch.

EVERYDAY USE
Tap an exercise, adjust the displayed weight, then Save. Leaving the last
weight unchanged and tapping Save records another entry. Tap the large weight
for an exact number. Step cycles through 0.5, 1, 2.5, 5 and 10. Convert to kg/lb
converts the entered value; it does not relabel old entries.

'Saved on watch / waiting for phone' means the entry is safe locally but the
phone has not yet confirmed it. Reconnect the paired devices and open both apps
to retry if needed. Full history and deletion are managed on the phone.
Do not uninstall or clear either app's storage while waiting for sync.

IF EXERCISES DO NOT APPEAR
- Confirm the phone shows version 1.8 and retains your saved weights.
- Open Lift Log on both devices, then tap the sync buttons.
- Check the watch is connected in the Galaxy Wearable app.
- Keep Google Play services enabled and current on both devices.
- If a device/app was force-stopped, reopen it to resume syncing.
- Do not uninstall as a troubleshooting step: that would remove local data.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
Both APKs compile and use the existing Lift Log signing key and package ID.
The phone retains its original history store and all 121 exercise identifiers.
Automated merge and storage tests cover retries, offline entries, older snapshots,
phone deletions, and concurrent phone edits. Native watch UI is tested with
Robolectric, not on a physical Galaxy Watch. Real paired-device transport and
Windows installation must still be checked on your devices.

Sync uses Google's Wear OS Data Layer. It normally uses the paired Bluetooth
connection and may use Google's encrypted relay when appropriate; it is not a
cloud backup. No Lift Log account, backend, health permissions, location or
microphone access is required.
